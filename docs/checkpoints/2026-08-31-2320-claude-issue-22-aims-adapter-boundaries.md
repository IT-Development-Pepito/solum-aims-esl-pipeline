- **Typed outcomes for reconciliation and retry:** `PageChangeOutcome` carries the domain's own `DeliveryCertainty` (FR-021) and `FailureSignal` (FR-015, architecture section 8) rather than a parallel vocabulary. Its invariants hold in both directions: a `CONFIRMED` outcome must carry a vendor receipt and must not carry a failure, and any non-confirmed outcome must carry a signal that `classify` can act on. An adapter therefore cannot claim unevidenced delivery, cannot report a failure the retry policy has no way to reason about, and never decides retry itself. An interrupted call stays representable as `UNKNOWN` instead of collapsing into success or failure.
