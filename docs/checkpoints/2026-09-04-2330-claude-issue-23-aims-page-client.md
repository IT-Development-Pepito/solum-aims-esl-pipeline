- **The distinction the design turns on.** Did the request arrive? Connection refused, connect timeout, and 502/503/504 mean it did not: `NOT_DELIVERED`, retryable. Read timeout, cut connection, `RemoteProtocolError`, 500, and a 200 whose body cannot be parsed mean it may already have moved a label: `UNKNOWN`, operator-action-required, never resent by the runner (FR-013). Collapsing the second into the first is how a page change silently happens twice, so the adapter never guesses in that direction. A vendor answer that declines, and any 4xx, is `REJECTION` and non-retryable.
